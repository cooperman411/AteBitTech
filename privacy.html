Privacy notice

Hosting and analytics

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. The live site also uses Cloudflare to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may process network and request information, including IP addresses.

The homepage and Price Guide use GoatCounter for traffic statistics. These can include pages visited, referrers, approximate location, browser and device information, screen width, and language. GoatCounter says its analytics do not use browser cookies. Its service uses IP and browser information temporarily in memory to count visits. The live site also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page performance.

Price Guide and eBay

When you search the Price Guide, your browser sends the search term and applicable marketplace and category to the Cloudflare-hosted pricing API. The API uses those search criteria to request current listings from the eBay Browse API. Your browser may load listing photos from eBay’s image servers.

The Price Guide includes eBay Partner Network affiliate links. If you buy through one of these links, I may earn a commission. When you open a listing, eBay receives the request and may use affiliate tracking and cookies as described in eBay’s privacy notice.

Appearance settings

If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser stores the choice in local storage as atebit-theme so it applies across the homepage, About, Privacy, and the Price Guide. “Use device setting” removes the saved choice.

Contact email

If you email me, I receive your email address and what you include in the message. I use that information to reply and handle your request. For privacy questions, email the site maintainer.
